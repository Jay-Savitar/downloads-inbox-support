Downloads Inbox Privacy Policy

November 24, 2026

Files and settings

Downloads Inbox processes filenames, file information, organization rules, folder permissions and operation history on your Mac so you can organize and restore files. The app does not send your files or organization history to the developer. No developer account is required.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Pro purchase. Downloads Inbox uses StoreKit to check purchase entitlements and handle restorations. The app stores a transaction reference locally to distinguish an unavailable verification from an account without a known purchase.

Your choices

You can pause monitoring and automatic organization, manage rules, and remove history records in the app. Removing a history record removes its restore shortcut. Your files remain on disk.

Support

If you contact support, share only the details needed to answer your question. Avoid sending personal files, account credentials or purchase receipts. Visit Downloads Inbox Support.
